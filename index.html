01THE LIVING SYSTEM

A watershed
connects us all.

Nairobi River is part of a wider network of streams and tributaries that flow through Kenya’s capital and onward to the Athi River system.

These waterways run beside homes, markets, roads, and green spaces. That closeness makes them vulnerable to pollution, but it also means local choices can make a difference.
